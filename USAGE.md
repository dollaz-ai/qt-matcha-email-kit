# Usage and attribution

Prepared for QT Matcha team collaboration. Brand marks, packaging, original illustrations, generated lifestyle imagery and design layouts are QT project assets. All rights reserved to their respective owners. Public repository visibility does not grant third parties a commercial license to use QT branding or assets.

Lifestyle images are generated brand creative selected for the website; do not represent them as customer submissions, testimonials or photographs documenting actual customers. Confirm packaging accuracy against final production artwork before campaign use.

The editable vector masters are Figma exports. Email-ready PNGs preserve those drawings without relying on a recipient’s SVG support. Photo JPEGs are derived from the approved WebP originals without retouching their content.

Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree are referenced by name and official Google Fonts links; font binaries are not redistributed in this pack. Review the font’s license if later bundling or self-hosting it.
