Keep the approved Soft Waves palette, typography and restrained illustration style while making these adjustments. Obtain team design review before rolling new changes across templates.

## Per-email checks

- Clear purpose, readable headline, one lead action; no placeholder text.
- Readable at 320/375/390 px and 600 px; no clipping, tiny CTA or unexpected image stretching.
- Wave colors match adjacent sections; no seams or text overlapping waves.
- Verify custom and fallback fonts, image-blocked appearance, dark mode, Apple Mail, Gmail and Outlook with actual inbox tests.
- Match flavor label, packet and lifestyle imagery; do not stretch, mirror or modify packaging print.
- Use meaningful photo alt text; decorative waves can have empty alt text.
- Check exported HTML size for Gmail clipping (roughly 102 KB); keep imagery reasonably compressed.
- Confirm all prices, inventory, promotions and policy statements. Validate QTCLUB15 in Shopify before sending the welcome offer.
- Review “the day we ship,” “doesn’t expire before then,” “first dibs” and “your spot is held” against actual launch operations.
- Set a real business mailing address and working unsubscribe for marketing. Check personalized fields and fallbacks without publishing customer data.
- For checkout emails, use the correct event recovery URL and purchase exclusions. For service emails, test actual Shopify notification variables and partial shipments.
- Confirm sender identity/domain setup with the account owner.
- Verify flow status and timing; repository publication is not email activation.
